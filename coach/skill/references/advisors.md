# Advisors

Use these as lenses, not roleplay characters. The goal is not mimicry; it is to extract distinct kinds of wisdom and let them productively disagree.

## Current advisor set

### Buddha lens
Focus on:
- suffering caused by craving, ego, attachment, and compulsive reactivity
- clarity through observation
- reducing self-created mental noise
- choosing the wiser, less ego-driven response

Useful questions:
- What desire or fear is disturbing the mind here?
- What would become easier if attachment dropped by 20%?
- Is this action rooted in awareness or compulsion?

### Shah Rukh Khan lens
Focus on:
- charisma, dignity, resilience, emotional courage, reinvention
- showing up with heart and self-belief
- living with style, energy, and human warmth rather than shrinking

Useful questions:
- Where is Jatin playing too small?
- What would the generous, confident version of him do?
- How can ambition and humanity coexist here?

### Jordan Peterson lens
Focus on:
- responsibility, order, honest self-confrontation, meaning through burden
- cleaning up chaos, telling the truth, carrying weight voluntarily

Useful questions:
- What responsibility is being avoided?
- What disorder needs to be put in order first?
- What truth is inconvenient but necessary?

### Elon Musk lens
Focus on:
- first-principles thinking, intensity, building, ambition, agency
- cutting through social scripts and asking what actually works
- bias toward execution and hard problems

Useful questions:
- What matters at the physics level, not the social-story level?
- Is the current pace too soft for the stated ambition?
- What would ruthless prioritization remove?

### Naval Ravikant lens
Focus on:
- leverage, judgment, specific knowledge, long-term compounding, peace
- choosing games worth playing and escaping noisy status traps

Useful questions:
- Is this effort compounding?
- What unique edge should Jatin lean into?
- Is this a wealth/peace move or a status distraction?

### Rick Rubin lens
Focus on:
- taste, creative honesty, subtraction, signal over noise
- making space to notice what is true before forcing output
- protecting sensitivity, intuition, and the conditions that let good work emerge

Useful questions:
- What is unnecessary here?
- What would make this feel more alive, more honest, more like you?
- Are you creating from instinct and attention, or from imitation and pressure?

## Shared maxim seeds
These came directly from Jatin’s framing and should influence the advice:
- Work hard on something you love.
- Do something you love until you love doing harder things.
- Use entry points that create deeper taste over time.
- Build a life where effort and meaning reinforce each other.

## Default weighting rule
Not every advisor should speak with equal weight on every topic.
Start by asking: who has the most signal for this specific domain?
Use that lens as the primary one, then bring in 1-2 adjacent or surprising lenses if they add something real.

Suggested default ordering by topic:
- **Creative work / taste / personal style / aesthetic decisions:** Rick Rubin → Shah Rukh Khan → Naval → Buddha
- **Discipline / responsibility / routine / self-honesty:** Jordan Peterson → Buddha → Naval → Musk
- **Ambition / building / execution / hard tradeoffs:** Elon Musk → Naval → Jordan Peterson → Shah Rukh Khan
- **Peace / detachment / ego / emotional turbulence:** Buddha → Naval → Shah Rukh Khan
- **Identity / presence / confidence / social expression:** Shah Rukh Khan → Rick Rubin → Buddha → Jordan Peterson
- **Career strategy / leverage / game selection:** Naval → Musk → Jordan Peterson → Rick Rubin
- **Meaning / long-arc life direction:** Buddha → Naval → Jordan Peterson → Rick Rubin

## Cross-domain spark rule
Do not keep advice trapped inside the obvious expert lane.
Sometimes the best insight comes from a lens that would not normally be chosen first.
Example: Jordan Peterson may still have something useful to say about dressing well because clothing can be framed as self-respect, order, and how you choose to meet the world.
Likewise, Rick Rubin may have something useful to say about work strategy because subtraction and signal-to-noise are often strategic, not just artistic.

So the rule is:
- choose a primary lens based on domain fit
- allow strong cross-domain lenses when they reveal something non-obvious
- avoid forcing every advisor into every answer

## Synthesis rule
When multiple lenses point the same way, say so clearly.
When they disagree, show the conflict plainly. Example:
- Buddha may say let go.
- Musk may say push harder.
- Naval may say choose a more leveraged game.
Then recommend the best fit for Jatin’s actual situation rather than pretending all three say the same thing.
