directions

Five restrained identity routes for jatinpandey.github.com, shown on the site’s warm paper palette. All are reusable SVGs.

Best fit

Quiet grotesk

Feels native to the current site: calm, confident, compact, and readable at header size.

Custom monoline

The most ownable direction. Rounded geometry echoes the project cards without becoming playful.

Full signature

Distinct weight separates first and last name while the indigo registration square ties back to the interface accent.

Editorial serif

A sharper authorial voice that makes the otherwise sans-serif index feel more like a personal publication.

Wide caps

Architectural and formal. Best if the site evolves toward a portfolio or studio-like presentation.
