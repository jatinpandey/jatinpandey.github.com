Hardwaremaxxing

I built a tiny side project that turns my phone into a remote trackpad for my laptop.

The setup is pretty simple: serve a local website from the laptop, open it on the phone, and use the touchscreen like a wireless mouse. It feels slightly ridiculous the first time you do it, and then immediately obvious.

It’s not a moonshot. It’s just a small, useful thing that probably didn’t need to exist — until it did.

Phone UI acting as a live trackpad for laptop control.

I think these are some of my favorite projects: small practical experiments that collapse the distance between “that would be nice” and “okay, done.”
