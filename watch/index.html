THE INSIDES OF A MECHANICAL WATCH

Time, taken apart.

A mechanical watch stores energy in a spring, passes it through gears, and releases it to the rhythm of an oscillating balance.

About the model

This is an original, simplified teaching model of a hand-wound Swiss lever movement, not a specific manufacturer’s calibre or manufacturing CAD. Gear speeds are linked; the escapement and springs use explanatory animation, not a force simulation. Exploded positions separate the parts for inspection. 1× represents a 2.5 Hz balance.
